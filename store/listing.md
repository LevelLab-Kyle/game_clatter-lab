## Description

Build a machine. Press Play. Watch it go!

In Clatter Lab, you build chain reaction machines. Drag gadgets onto the board. Then press Play. Get anything to the star to win.

LEVELS
• 75 puzzles in 3 worlds.
• Each level gives you a few parts.
• Each new gadget gets its own level.
• Stuck? Tap the ? button for help.

SANDBOX
• Use every gadget. No limits.
• Try the ready-made examples.

GADGETS
Balls, marbles, dominoes, planks, ramps, blocks, buckets, seesaws, bouncers, conveyors, fans, launchers, pipes, spinners, hammers, pendulums, pulleys and bells.

MADE FOR KIDS
• No ads.
• No in-app purchases.
• No accounts. No chat.
• Works with no internet.

Made by Level Lab for kids in grades K-8.
